The ContextWindow

A standing morning call for people who build with AI. Remote, small, technical. You bring what you made or what broke. Together we hold more of it than one head can.

One head can't hold all of this.

The field moves faster than anyone can read. Models change under you, tools ship weekly, and the thing that worked in March is wrong by June. A model has a fixed context window. So do you. The only way to hold more is to share it.

So we meet. In the morning, before the day fills up, on a call with people who are actually building.
