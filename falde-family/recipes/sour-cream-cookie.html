Sour Cream Cookies

A big rolled sour-cream cookie, brushed and sugared.

Credited to Mrs. J., headed “(double recipe)” — 7 cups of flour.

Ingredients

  • 5eggs (3 whites saved for the top)
  • 4 csugar
  • ½ lbbutter
  • 1 ptsour cream
  • 1 tsalt
  • 2 tsoda (rounding)
  • 1 tnutmeg
  • 7 cflour
  • 2 tbaking powder
  • 3 tlemon extract

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar and add the well-beaten eggs (keeping 3 whites aside for the top).
  2. Add the sour cream and the dry ingredients — soda, nutmeg, flour, baking powder, salt and lemon extract — with a little more flour so it will roll.
  3. Roll and cut. Brush with the reserved egg whites and sprinkle with sugar.
  4. Bake in a moderate oven (about 375 °F).
