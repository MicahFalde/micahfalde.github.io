Date Bread

A graham quick bread with dates or raisins.

In pencil, in her hand.

Ingredients

  • 2 cbrown sugar
  • ½ cshortening (scant)
  • 2eggs
  • 1 tsalt
  • 2 cgraham flour
  • 2 cwhite flour
  • 2 csour or buttermilk
  • 2 tsoda
  • 1 cdates or raisins
  • —nuts

Method

  1. Mix in the order given.
  2. Pour into greased loaf pans and bake in a moderate oven (about 350 °F) about an hour.

The card says only “mix in order given” — the bake is the usual one for a quick-bread loaf of this size (it makes two).
