Corn Meal Bread

A yeast-raised cornmeal loaf, sweetened with molasses.

In her hand; the kneading and bake finish on the back.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cwater
  • 1 tsalt (plus more)
  • ⅓ cyellow cornmeal
  • ⅓ cmolasses
  • 1½ tsalad oil or melted shortening
  • 1 pkgdry yeast
  • ¼ clukewarm water
  • 2¼ cwhite flour

Method

  1. Bring 1½ cups water and 1 t salt to a boil. Add the cornmeal and bring to a boil again, stirring constantly.
  2. Stir in the molasses and the salad oil or melted shortening. Let cool to lukewarm.
  3. Dissolve the yeast in ¼ cup lukewarm water for 10 minutes; add to the cornmeal and mix well. Add the white flour (and more as needed).
  4. Knead, let rise, shape into a loaf, and bake in a moderate oven (about 375 °F) until done. The kneading and bake run onto the back of the card.

The mixing continues on the back with the usual knead-rise-bake finish for a yeast loaf.
