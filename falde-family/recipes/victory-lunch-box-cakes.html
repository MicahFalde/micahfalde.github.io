Victory Lunch-Box Cakes

A wartime cupcake batter, split to make two kinds at once.

In her hand; the “Victory” name and the split-batter trick date it to the war years. The method finishes on the back.

Ingredients

  • 2 csifted flour
  • 2 tbaking powder
  • ½ tsalt
  • ¾ csugar
  • ½ cshortening
  • 1egg, well beaten
  • ¾ cmilk
  • 1 tvanilla
  • 2 Tcocoa + 1 t cinnamonher corner note, to spice one half

Method

  1. Sift the flour once, measure, add the baking powder, salt and sugar, and sift together three times.
  2. Cream the shortening. Add the dry ingredients, egg, milk and vanilla. Stir until the flour is dampened, then beat vigorously 1 minute.
  3. Divide the batter in halves to make two kinds — she notes 2 T cocoa and 1 t cinnamon in the corner, to turn one half chocolate-spice.
  4. Fill lined pans and bake in a moderate oven (about 375 °F) until done, about 20 minutes.

The method runs onto the back, where the two variations are finished. The bake given here is the usual one for cupcakes; the front doesn’t state it.
