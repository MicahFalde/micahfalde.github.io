Fruit Torte

A shortbread crust under fruit, custard and meringue.

In her hand, in faded pencil.

  • ¼ csugarmeringue

Method

  1. Cream the butter and 6 T sugar well; add the flour. Pat into a shallow pan and bake in a medium oven.
  2. Lay fruit over the baked crust — apricots, peach, apple, pineapple, or fresh strawberries.
  3. Cook the egg yolks, milk, 3 T sugar and cornstarch in a double boiler until thick; spread over the fruit.
  4. Cover with a meringue of the 3 egg whites beaten with ¼ cup sugar, and brown in the oven.

The card is in faded pencil and gives no exact oven temperature; a “medium oven” is about 350 °F. This is the actual Fruit Torte named in her canned-fruit chart.

Fruit Torte (or Torte)
½# butter · 6 tbs. sugar · 2 cups flour — cream well & add flour. Pat in shallow pan & bake in medium oven. Place on this fruit — apricots, peach, apple, pineapple, fresh strawberries.
3 egg yolks · 1½ cups milk · 3 tbs. sugar · 1 tb. cornstarch — cook in double boiler until thick, spread on top of fruit. Cover with meringue, 3 egg whites & ¼ cup sugar. Brown in oven.
