Macaroon Pie

A cracker-crumb, date and nut macaroon, baked in a pie tin.

In pencil, in her hand.

Ingredients

  • 3egg whites
  • 1 csugar
  • ¾ ccracker crumbs
  • ½ cnuts
  • ½ cdates

Method

  1. Beat the egg whites; add the sugar.
  2. Fold in the cracker crumbs, nuts and dates.
  3. Bake in a pie tin 30 minutes at 300 °F. Serve with whipped cream.

As written

Macaroon Pie
3 egg whites · 1 c. sugar · ¾ c. cracker crumbs · ½ c nuts · ½ c dates (mix)

Beat egg whites, add sugar, fold in rest. Bake in pie tin 30 min at 300°. Serve with whipped cream.
