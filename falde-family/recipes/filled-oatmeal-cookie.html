Filled Oatmeal Cookies

Rolled oatmeal cookies with a cooked raisin filling between.

In her hand; the mixing finishes on the back.

Ingredients

  • 2½–3 cflour
  • 3 coatmeal
  • 1 csugar
  • ½ csour milk
  • 1 cshortening
  • 1 tcinnamon
  • 1 tsoda
  • —salt
  • 1egg
  • 1 lbraisinsfilling
  • ½ csugarfilling
  • 1 Tflourfilling
  • 1 cwaterfilling

Method

  1. Grind the oatmeal. Add the dry ingredients and cut in the shortening; add the sour milk (with the soda) and the egg to make a dough.
  2. Roll thin, cut in rounds, and bake in a moderate oven (about 375 °F).
  3. For the filling, cook the raisins, sugar, flour and water until thick, stirring as it cooks; cool.
  4. When cold, spread the filling between two cookies.

The dough-mixing runs onto the back of the card. The roll-and-bake step and temperature are the usual ones for a filled rolled cookie.
