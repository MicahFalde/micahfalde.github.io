Quicky Chocolate Cookies

A no-bake chocolate-marshmallow square.

In her hand, in blue ink.

Ingredients

  • 1 pkgchocolate chips
  • 1 large pkgmarshmallows (42)
  • 3 Tbutter
  • 1½ cRice Krispies
  • ½ tsalt
  • ½ cchopped nuts

Method

  1. Melt the first three ingredients in a double boiler.
  2. Add the cereal, salt and nuts.
  3. Press into a buttered pan and cut in squares when cold.
