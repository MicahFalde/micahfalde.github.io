Charmin’ Cherry Bars

A shortbread base under a cherry, coconut and walnut top.

In her hand, front and back. Makes 3 dozen.

Ingredients

  • 1 csifted flourcrust
  • ¼ cpowdered sugarcrust
  • ½ cbuttercrust
  • ¼ cflourtopping
  • ½ tbaking powdertopping
  • ¼ tsalttopping
  • ¾ csugartopping
  • 2eggs, slightly beatentopping
  • ½ cmaraschino cherries, finely cuttopping
  • ½ ccoconuttopping
  • ½ cchopped walnutstopping

Method

  1. For the crust, sift together 1 cup flour and ¼ cup powdered sugar. Cut in the butter until the mixture resembles coarse meal. Press into an ungreased pan (about 7 × 11 or 9 × 9) and bake in a moderate oven 10 minutes.
  2. For the topping, sift together ¼ cup flour, ½ t baking powder and ¼ t salt; stir in the sugar. Add the slightly beaten eggs.
  3. Fold in the cherries, coconut and walnuts, and spread over the partially baked dough.
  4. Bake in a moderate oven 30–40 minutes. Cool and cut into bars. Makes 3 dozen.
