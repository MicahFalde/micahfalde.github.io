Seven Minute Frosting

The classic beaten-over-water frosting.

Credited on the card to Mrs. J., with a marshmallow variation from Beatrice.

Ingredients

  • ¾ csugar, or 1 cup brown sugar
  • 3 Twater
  • 1egg white

Method

  1. Combine the sugar, water and egg white in the top of a double boiler.
  2. Beat over boiling water for seven minutes, until it stands in peaks.

Beatrice’s variation: after removing from the fire, add 12 marshmallows, ½ cup pineapple juice and vanilla.

As written

Seven Minute Frosting (Mrs. J.)
¾ c sugar or 1 cup brown sugar · 3 T water · 1 egg white

Beat in double boiler seven minutes.
May add after removing from fire (Beatrice): 12 marshmallows, ½ cup pineapple juice, vanilla.
