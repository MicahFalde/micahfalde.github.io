Refrigerator Whole Wheat Rolls

An icebox dough to mix ahead and bake fresh.

In blue ink that has bled with a water stain; the shaping finishes on the back.

Ingredients

  • 1 cakeyeast
  • ½ clukewarm water
  • 1egg
  • 2 tsalt
  • ½ cshortening
  • 2 cscalded milk
  • ¾ cbrown sugar
  • 4 cwhole wheat flour, plus white flour

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm water. Add the sugar, shortening and salt to the scalded milk and cool to room temperature.
  2. Combine the liquids and egg, then stir in the whole wheat flour. Add enough white flour to make a soft dough.
  3. Let rise until doubled; knead 10 minutes. Cover with a damp cloth and waxed paper and place in the refrigerator.
  4. When wanted, shape into rolls, let rise, and bake in a hot oven (about 400 °F) 15–20 minutes. The shaping finishes on the back of the card.
