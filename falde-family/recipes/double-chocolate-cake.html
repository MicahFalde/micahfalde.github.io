Double Chocolate Cake

A one-bowl chocolate cake, reddened with a little food coloring.

In pencil, faded. The method and a “Chocolate Icing Deluxe” run onto the back.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ cflour
  • 1½ csugar
  • 1¼ tdouble-action baking powder
  • ½ tsoda
  • 1 tsalt
  • ½ cshortening
  • 1 cmilk
  • 1 tvanilla
  • ½–⅔ cunbeaten eggs (2)
  • 2 ozunsweetened chocolate
  • ½ tred food coloring

Method

  1. Put the flour, sugar, baking powder, soda, salt, shortening, milk and vanilla in the bowl. Mix with an electric mixer on slow to medium speed for 2 minutes, scraping the bowl often.
  2. Add the eggs, chocolate and food coloring and beat 2 more minutes.
  3. Bake 30–35 minutes at 375 °F.
  4. For the Chocolate Icing Deluxe, beat until fluffy: 1 large unbeaten egg, 2 cups sifted confectioners’ sugar, ¼ t salt, ⅓ cup soft shortening, and 2 squares (2 oz) unsweetened chocolate, melted.

As written

Double chocolate cake
1¾ c. flour · 1½ c. sugar · 1¼ tsp. double-action baking powder · ½ tsp. soda · 1 tsp. salt · ½ c. shortening · 1 c milk · 1 tsp. vanilla · ½ to ⅔ c. unbeaten eggs (2) · 2 oz. unsweetened chocolate · ½ tsp. red food coloring

Mix with electric mixer on slow to medium speed for 2 min. Scrape sides and bottom of bowl frequently. Add second dish of ingredients & beat 2 more min. Bake 30–35 min at 375°.
Chocolate icing deluxe — Beat with spoon or electric mixer until fluffy: 1 large unbeaten egg, 2 cups sifted confectioners' sugar, ¼ tsp. salt, ⅓ c. soft shortening, 2 sq. (2 oz.) unsweetened chocolate, melted.
