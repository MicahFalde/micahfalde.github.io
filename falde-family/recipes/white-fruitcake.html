White Fruitcake

A pale fruitcake of citron, candied cherries and pineapple.

In her hand; the method finishes on the back.

Ingredients

  • ½ clard
  • 1 csugar
  • 4egg whites
  • 1 tbaking powder
  • ¼ tsalt
  • ½ cchopped citron
  • ½ cchopped candied cherries
  • ½ cchopped candied pineapple
  • ¼ cchopped walnuts
  • 2 cflour

Method

  1. Cream the shortening; add the sugar gradually, mixing well. Add the unbeaten egg whites one at a time, beating well after each.
  2. Sift the flour, measure, and sift one cup with the baking powder and salt. Add the remaining cup of flour to the chopped fruits.
  3. Add the sifted flour to the batter, then fold in the floured fruit and the walnuts.
  4. Bake in a slow oven (about 300 °F) until done. The method finishes on the back of the card.
