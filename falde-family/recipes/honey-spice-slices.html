Honey-Spice Slices

A sweet yeast loaf with a honey-cinnamon top, sliced.

In her hand, with the temperature, pan and time noted right on the card.

Ingredients

  • 3 Tsugardough
  • ¾ tsaltdough
  • 3 Tshorteningdough
  • ½ cmilk, scaldeddough
  • 1 cakeyeastdough
  • ¼ clukewarm waterdough
  • 1egg, well beatendough
  • 3 cflourdough
  • 2 Tbutter, meltedtopping
  • 3 Tsugartopping
  • ¾ tcinnamontopping
  • 2 Tstrained honeytopping

Method

  1. Add the sugar, salt and shortening to the cooled scalded milk. Soften the yeast in the lukewarm water and add to the milk mixture.
  2. Beat in the egg and the flour to make a dough; let rise.
  3. Shape into a 9 × 5 × 3 loaf, brush with the honey-spice topping (melted butter, sugar, cinnamon and honey), and let rise.
  4. Bake at 375 °F for 35 to 40 minutes.

The mixing runs onto the back of the card. She noted the bake right on the front: 375 °F, a 9 × 5 × 3 pan, 35–40 minutes.
