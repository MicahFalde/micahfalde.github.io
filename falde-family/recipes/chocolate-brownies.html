Chocolate Brownies

A plain, nut-topped brownie.

Credited on the card to Mrs. S.

Ingredients

  • 2 sqchocolate
  • ⅓ cfat
  • 2eggs
  • ¼ tbaking powder
  • ½ cflour
  • 1 csugar
  • 1 tvanilla
  • ⅛ tsalt
  • ½ cchopped nuts

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate and fat together. Stir in the beaten eggs and sugar.
  2. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt and add to the others. Stir in the vanilla.
  3. Put in a greased pan ½ inch thick and sprinkle the nuts over the top.
  4. Bake in a moderate oven (about 350 °F) and cut in squares.

The last line of the method runs onto the back; a moderate oven, about 350 °F for 25–30 minutes, is the usual bake.
