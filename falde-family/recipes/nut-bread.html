Nut Bread

A plain baking-powder nut bread.

Typed on the card, in her file.

Ingredients

  • 2 cflour
  • ⅓ csugar
  • 3 tbaking powder
  • ½ tsalt
  • ½ cnut meats
  • 1 cmilk
  • 2 Tshortening
  • 1egg

Method

  1. Sift the dry ingredients together. Add the egg and milk, and the shortening, melted and cooled.
  2. Break the nut meats coarsely and fold into the mixture.
  3. Bake in a deep, well-greased pan in a moderate oven for 40 to 45 minutes.

As written

NUT BREAD
2 c. flour · ⅓ c. sugar · 3 t. baking powder · ½ t. salt · ½ c. nut meats · 1 c. milk · 2 T. shortening · 1 egg

Sift dry ingredients together. Add egg and milk, and shortening melted and cooled. Break nut meats coarsely and fold into mixture. Bake in deep pan, well greased, in moderate oven for 40 to 45 minutes.
