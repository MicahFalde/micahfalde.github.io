Butter Brickle Frosting

A browned-butter frosting.

In pencil, in her hand; the last line runs onto the back.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cbutter
  • 2 csifted confectioners’ sugar
  • 2 Tcream
  • 1½ tvanilla
  • 2 Thot water

Method

  1. Melt the butter and keep over low heat until golden brown. Remove from the heat.
  2. Blend in the sugar, cream and vanilla. Stir in the hot water.
  3. Stir vigorously until cool and of a spreading consistency (if it gets too stiff, add a little more hot water).

As written

Butter Brickle Frosting
¼ c. butter · 2 c. sifted confectioners' sugar · 2 tbsp. cream · 1½ tsp. vanilla · 2 tbsp. hot water

Melt butter and keep over low heat until golden brown. Remove from heat. Blend in sugar, cream, vanilla. Stir in hot water. Stir vigorously until cool and of a consistency to spread (if it gets too stiff, add a little more hot water).
