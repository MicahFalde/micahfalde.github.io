Whole Wheat Nut Bread

A molasses whole-wheat quick bread with walnuts.

Filed under the tab, headed “(home).” The bake finishes on the back.

Ingredients

  • 2 cwhole wheat flour
  • 1 cbread flour
  • 1 tsoda
  • 3 tbaking powder
  • 2 tsalt
  • ½ cwater
  • 5 Tbrown sugar
  • ½ cmolasses
  • ¾ cmilk
  • 1 cchopped walnuts
  • 2 Tmelted Crisco

Method

  1. Mix the whole wheat flour, bread flour, soda, baking powder, salt and sugar.
  2. Add the molasses, milk and water and mix well.
  3. Add the nuts and Crisco and beat thoroughly.
  4. Pour into a greased pan and bake in a moderate oven (about 350 °F) about an hour. The bake finishes on the back of the card.
