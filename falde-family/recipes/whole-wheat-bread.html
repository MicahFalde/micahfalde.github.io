Whole Wheat Bread

A molasses whole-wheat yeast loaf.

In blue ink, marked by a coffee-cup ring in the middle of the card.

Ingredients

  • 2 cakesyeast
  • ¾ clukewarm water
  • 1 qtmilk, scalded
  • 3 tsalt
  • ¼ csugar
  • 2 Tmolasses
  • 2 Tlard
  • 3 cwhole wheat flour, plus white flour

Method

  1. Scald and cool the milk; pour over the whole wheat flour and mix. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm water.
  2. Add the yeast, salt, sugar, molasses, lard, and enough white flour to make a dough.
  3. Let rise until double (about 3½ hours), then again — but do not let it rise as light as white bread.
  4. Bake at 375–400 °F for 45 minutes.

As written

Whole wheat Bread
2 cakes yeast dissolved in ¾ c. lukewarm water · 1 qt. milk scalded · 3 tsp salt · ¼ c. sugar · 2 Tbls. molasses · 2 Tbls. lard · 3 c. whole wheat & white [flour]

Cool milk, pour over whole wheat. Mix. Add rest. Raise until double (3½ hours), then again. Do not let rise as light as white. Bake 375°–400°, 45 min.
