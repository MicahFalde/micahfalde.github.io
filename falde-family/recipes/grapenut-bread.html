Grapenut Bread

Grapenuts soaked soft in buttermilk.

In pencil, in her hand.

Ingredients

  • 1 cGrapenuts
  • 2 cbuttermilk
  • 1 csugar
  • 2eggs
  • ½ tsalt
  • 4 cflour
  • 1 tbaking powder
  • 1 tsoda

Method

  1. Soak the Grapenuts in the buttermilk ½ to 1 hour.
  2. Combine everything in the order given.
  3. Bake 1 hour at 325 °F.
