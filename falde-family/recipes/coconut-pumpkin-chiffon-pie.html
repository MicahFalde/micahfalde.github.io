Coconut Pumpkin Chiffon Pie

A light gelatin pumpkin pie under toasted coconut.

A Baker’s Coconut (General Foods) leaflet she saved.

Ingredients

  • 1 env (1 T)gelatin
  • ¼ ccold water
  • 1¼ cmashed cooked pumpkin
  • ¾ cevaporated milk
  • ½ cwater
  • 2egg yolks, slightly beaten
  • ¾ cbrown sugar, packed
  • ½ tsalt
  • ¼ tginger
  • ½ tnutmeg
  • ½ tcinnamon
  • 2egg whites
  • ½ tvanilla
  • 1 cBaker’s Shredded Coconut, toasted
  • 1baked 9-inch pie shell
  • ⅓ ccream, whipped and sweetened

Method

  1. Soften the gelatin in the cold water. In the top of a double boiler, combine the pumpkin, milk, water, egg yolks, ½ cup of the sugar, the salt and spices. Cook over boiling water 10 minutes, stirring constantly.
  2. Add the gelatin and stir until dissolved. Remove from the heat and chill until slightly thickened.
  3. Beat the egg whites until foamy; add the remaining sugar gradually and beat until stiff. Fold in the pumpkin mixture, the vanilla, and ¾ of the toasted coconut.
  4. Turn into the cold pie shell and chill until firm. Before serving, top with the whipped cream and the rest of the toasted coconut. (To toast coconut: spread thinly in a shallow pan and toast in a moderate oven, 350 °F, about 10 minutes.)
