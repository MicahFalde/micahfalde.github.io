Kellogg-Quick All-Bran Muffins

The All-Bran box muffin — with a jam variation.

A cereal-box panel she saved.

Ingredients

  • 1 cKellogg’s All-Bran
  • ¾ cmilk
  • 1egg
  • ¼ csoft shortening
  • 1 csifted flour
  • 2½ tbaking powder
  • ½ tsalt
  • ¼ csugar

Method

  1. Combine the All-Bran and milk; let stand until most of the moisture is taken up. Add the egg and shortening; beat well.
  2. Sift together the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar. Add to the first mixture, stirring only until combined. Fill greased muffin pans two-thirds full.
  3. Bake in a moderately hot oven (400 °F) about 30 minutes. Makes 9 muffins.
  4. For Jam-Bran Muffins, press a teaspoon of jam lightly into the top of each muffin just before baking.
