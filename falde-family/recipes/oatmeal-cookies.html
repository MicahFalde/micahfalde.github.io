Method

  1. Cream the shortening and sugar; add the eggs and the sour milk.
  2. Add the dry ingredients, then the raisins and oatmeal.
  3. Drop on a sheet and bake in a moderate oven (about 375 °F).

The card is a faded pencil ingredient list with no method, and the oatmeal quantity is worn away at the bottom edge (2 to 3 cups is usual for this size batch). The mixing above is the standard drop-cookie order.
