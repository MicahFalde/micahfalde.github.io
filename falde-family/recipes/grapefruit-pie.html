Grapefruit Pie

A grapefruit custard under meringue.

In her hand, spattered but legible; the method finishes on the back.

Ingredients

  • 1 cwater
  • 1 csugar
  • 3 Tcornstarch
  • —a few grains of salt
  • ¾ cgrapefruit juice
  • 2egg yolks
  • 2egg whitesmeringue
  • ¼ csugarmeringue
  • 1baked pie shell

Method

  1. Boil the sugar, water and ½ cup of the grapefruit juice.
  2. Mix the cornstarch and salt with the remaining ¼ cup juice and stir into the boiling water. When thick, remove from the fire and stir in the egg yolks.
  3. Cook a moment more, pour into a baked shell.
  4. Beat the egg whites with ¼ cup sugar to a meringue, spread over the pie, and brown in a moderate oven (about 350 °F).

The custard-cooking runs onto the back of the card; the meringue-and-brown finish is the usual one.
