Chocolate Angel Food

An eleven-white angel food, darkened with cocoa.

Credited on the card to Mrs. S. She wrote the ingredients only.

Ingredients

  • 11egg whites (1¼ c)
  • 1¼ cfine sugar, sifted
  • ¾ ccake flour, sifted 5 times
  • ¼ ccocoa, sifted with the flour
  • 1 tcream of tartar
  • ¼ tsalt
  • 1 tvanilla

Method

  1. Beat the egg whites with the cream of tartar and salt until they hold soft peaks.
  2. Add the sugar gradually, then fold in the sifted flour and cocoa a little at a time. Fold in the vanilla.
  3. Turn into an ungreased tube pan and bake at 350 °F about 45 minutes. Invert to cool.

The card is an ingredient list. The method above is the standard angel-food one, matching her plain Angel Food in the same box; the card itself gives no method or temperature.
