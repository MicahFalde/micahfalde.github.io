Soft Graham Bread

A yeast-raised graham loaf.

Credited on the card to Mrs. S.

Ingredients

  • 3 cwarm water
  • 2 Tmelted butter
  • 2 tsalt
  • 1 cakeyeast
  • ¼ cwater
  • ¾ cbrown sugar
  • 1½ qtgraham flour
  • 1 ptwhite flour

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the ¼ cup water. Combine with the warm water, butter, salt, brown sugar and flours.
  2. Mix and pour into greased tins.
  3. Let it rise, then bake (a moderate oven, about 375 °F, 40–45 minutes).

The bake temperature isn’t written; a graham yeast loaf bakes around 375 °F.
