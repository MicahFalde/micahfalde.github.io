Upside Down Cake

Apples or peaches caramelized in the pan, cake baked over them.

Filed under the tab “Upside down cake,” headed “(home).”

Ingredients

  • 3 TCriscotopping
  • 1 cbrown sugartopping
  • 6soft ripe apples or peaches, peeled and slicedtopping
  • 1egg, beatenbatter
  • ½ cbrown sugarbatter
  • ¼ tsaltbatter
  • ½ chot milkbatter
  • 1 cflourbatter
  • 1½ tbaking powderbatter

Method

  1. In a cold frying pan, put the Crisco and 1 cup brown sugar and stir over a slow fire until melted. Add the sliced apples or peaches.
  2. For the batter, mix the egg, ½ cup brown sugar and salt; add the hot milk, then the flour and baking powder.
  3. Pour the batter over the fruit and bake in a moderate oven (about 350 °F).
  4. Turn out upside down onto a plate.
