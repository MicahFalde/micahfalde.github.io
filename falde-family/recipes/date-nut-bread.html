Date-Nut Bread

Dates and nuts steeped in boiling water, then baked.

In her hand, in pencil. (The scan came labeled “Oatnut,” but the card is headed Date nut Bread.)

Ingredients

  • 1 cchopped dates
  • ¾ cnut meats
  • 1 cboiling water
  • 1½ tsoda
  • 2eggs
  • 1 Tshortening
  • ½ csugar
  • 1½ cflour

Method

  1. Put the nuts and dates, soda and shortening in a bowl and add the boiling water.
  2. Beat the eggs and sugar; add the flour alternately with the date mixture.
  3. Bake at 350 °F for 1 hour.
