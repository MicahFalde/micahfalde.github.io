Pumpkin Bread

Two spiced pumpkin loaves.

In pencil, faded and water-stained down the left edge.

Ingredients

  • 3 csugar
  • 1 cshortening
  • 2 c (1 lb)pumpkin
  • ⅔ cwater
  • 3⅓ csifted flour
  • 2 tsoda
  • 1½ tsalt
  • 1½ tpie spice

Method

  1. Cream the sugar and shortening. Add the pumpkin and water, then the dry ingredients.
  2. Divide between 2 loaf pans and bake at 350 °F for 55–60 minutes.
